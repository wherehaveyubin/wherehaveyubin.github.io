Paper Review — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Paper Review is a desktop application for managing a personal library of academic papers. This policy explains what it does with your information, including data it receives from Google APIs.

Data the app accesses

When you connect Paper Review to Google Drive, it requests access to your Google Drive files. It uses this access only to read and write the files of your Paper Review library — the library database, the PDF papers, and their supporting files — in the Drive folder you choose for the library.

How the data is used

The app downloads your library from Google Drive to your computer when it opens and uploads your changes back to the same folder when it closes or when you ask it to sync. That is the only purpose: keeping your library the same on your own devices. The app does not read, change or delete Drive files outside the library folder.

Where the data goes

Data moves only between your Google Drive and your own computer. Paper Review has no server of its own. It does not send your Google data to the developer or to anyone else, does not sell it, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any machine-learning model. The sign-in token Google issues is stored on your computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI or the macOS Keychain).

AI features

Some features (summaries, questions about a paper) send the text of a paper you choose to Anthropic's Claude, through the Claude Code tool installed on your computer and signed in with your own Anthropic account. These requests run only when you use those features, and they are governed by your agreement with Anthropic.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Paper Review's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings, and revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files stay in your Drive.
